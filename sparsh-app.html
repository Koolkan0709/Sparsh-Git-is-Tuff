Sparsh

The warning your feet can no longer give you.

Nerve damage takes away the ache that makes everyone else shift their weight. Sparsh watches how long each part of your foot stays loaded, and tells you when it is time to move — before the skin has anything to show.

Takes about two minutes. Your details stay in this browser tab.
